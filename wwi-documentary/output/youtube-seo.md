What it says (title judgement, not proof of cause):
- **"Wrong turn" is a proven angle.** Three different channels used almost the same title and all beat or matched their
  channel (OER 34x, HISTORY 12.5x, Zem TV 1.25M). Viewers already search for this exact story.
- **Age is a hook.** "The Teenager Who Started World War I" is the biggest outlier in the niche (98x). Princip was 19.
- **Shorts on this exact twist are thin.** Search returns very few English Shorts on the wrong turn itself; the big Short
  is a general cartoon (Simple History, 65x). The long-form demand is proven and the Short format is open.
- **Avoid the sandwich myth.** "Princip ate a sandwich" titles are small (0.11M total views) and the story is a myth
  (no primary source; it comes from a 2001 novel). Our script leaves it out.

In 1914, a driver's wrong turn put Archduke Franz Ferdinand five feet from his assassin, and started World War I.

28 June 1914, Sarajevo. A bomb thrown at the Archduke's car missed and wounded around twenty people. After a reception at the Town Hall, Franz Ferdinand decided to visit the wounded in hospital. The new route went straight along the Appel Quay, but nobody told his driver. At the Latin Bridge the car turned into Franz Josef Street, stopped, and stalled while reversing, right beside 19-year-old Gavrilo Princip. Two shots killed the Archduke and his wife Sophie. One month later Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia; within a week Russia, Germany, France and Britain were drawn in. World War I killed an estimated 15 to 22 million people.

## Fact notes (changes from the draft script)
- "History's deadliest war" → "One of history's deadliest wars": World War II was deadlier.
- The bomb wounded **16–20** people (not "dozens").
- Princip had **not given up**: after the bomb failed he moved near Schiller's delicatessen to wait for the return trip.
  The script says "he had missed his chance that morning", which is accurate.
- Driver: Leopold Lojka followed the first two cars into Franz Josef Street; Governor Potiorek, riding in the car,
  called out that it was the wrong way; the engine stalled as he tried to reverse.
- The first bullet hit the Archduke (neck), the second Sophie (abdomen); both were dead by 11:30 a.m.
- Deaths: estimates run 15–22 million; the narration says "an estimated 20 million" and the screen shows the range.
- Sandwich story: a myth, left out.
