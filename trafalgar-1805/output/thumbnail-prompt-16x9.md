# Thumbnail prompt (16:9): Battle of Trafalgar Documentary: Nelson's Last Battle (1805)

Works in ChatGPT, Google Flow (Imagen) or Ideogram. Set **16:9 (landscape)**.

## Prompt (with text)
```
Create a horizontal 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a history documentary, photorealistic and cinematic, like a still from a big-budget war film. Warm sunset gold light against dark blue-grey sea and thick white cannon smoke.

Scene: the Battle of Trafalgar, 1805. On the right, the huge wooden three-deck British flagship HMS Victory, black and yellow hull, all sails set, firing a full broadside: bright orange muzzle flashes along all three gun decks, smoke billowing. It is cutting straight through a line of French and Spanish warships; on the left, an enemy ship with shattered masts is burning. Splinters and spray in the air. Low camera angle at water level, dramatic, epic scale.

Text, perfectly spelled, bold condensed sans-serif capitals with a heavy black drop shadow, on the left third of the image:
- Huge, two lines: "LOST:" in white, "0 SHIPS" in gold.
- Under it, a small black box with a thin gold border and white text: "27 vs 33".

Keep the right half (the flagship and the explosion) free of text. High contrast, sharp, clean typography. No other words, no watermark, no logo, no modern ships.
```

## Variant B: with Nelson
```
Create a horizontal 16:9 YouTube thumbnail, photorealistic and cinematic. Left side: Admiral Horatio Nelson in 1805, pale face, grey hair, black bicorne hat, dark blue admiral's coat with gold epaulettes and four glittering star medals, his right sleeve pinned empty, standing on the smoky quarterdeck of HMS Victory, calm and determined, lit by orange cannon fire. Right side: a line of enemy sailing warships exploding in broadsides behind thick white smoke. Text top-right, bold condensed capitals with black shadow: "LOST:" white, "0 SHIPS" gold. No other words, no watermark, no logo.
```
(Nelson lost his right arm in 1797, so the empty right sleeve is historically accurate.)

## Short version (if the tool simplifies too much)
```
16:9 cinematic photo: HMS Victory, a three-deck 1805 warship, firing a huge broadside through a line of enemy ships at sunset, white smoke, orange flashes, a burning ship on the left. Text on the left: "LOST:" (white) "0 SHIPS" (gold), small box "27 vs 33". Only these words, spelled exactly. No watermark.
```

## Negative prompt (if supported)
```
misspelled text, extra words, gibberish letters, watermark, logo, modern ship, steel hull, smokestack, cartoon, painting style, blurry, deformed
```

## Tips
- Thumbnail text **"LOST: 0 SHIPS"** pairs with the title **"Battle of Trafalgar Documentary: Nelson's Last Battle (1805)"** (vidIQ 95) without repeating any of its words.
- If a word is misspelled, reply: *"Keep everything the same, only fix the text to read exactly: LOST: 0 SHIPS"*.
- HMS Victory's real 1805 paint scheme is **black with yellow-ochre bands along the gun decks** (the "Nelson chequer"). Ask for it if the ship looks generic.
