## Summary
On the afternoon of Tuesday, 24 November 2026, a single-engine Cessna 210 carrying six people crashed into
the water between Pascagoula and Horn Island, Mississippi, part of Gulf Islands National Seashore. The plane
had left New Orleans Lakefront Airport for Page Field in Fort Myers, Florida; reports say the family aboard was
returning home from a fishing trip in Louisiana. The FAA said the aircraft was descending at about 10,000 feet
per minute. Responders first found three life jackets, a life raft and the plane's cabin floating; after a
search of about four hours, Mississippi Department of Marine Resources divers found all six inside the
fuselage. The NTSB is leading the investigation with the FAA. No cause has been announced.

## Conflicting / uncertain figures
- **Ages and how many children:** Early Coast Guard figures said "four adults and two boys, ages 8 and 10" [1].
  Later identifications list a 16-year-old, a 12-year-old and a 10-year-old [7][10]; one outlet says "three children" [11].
  The pilot's age is given as 50 [1] and 42 [10].
