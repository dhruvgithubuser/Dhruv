## Description

```
In 1944, two French cyclists watched four American soldiers pick up a Sherman tank. The tank was a balloon — it weighed 93 pounds.

This is the Ghost Army (23rd Headquarters Special Troops): 1,100 Americans, many of them artists recruited from art schools and ad agencies, whose job was to make the Germans see an army of 30,000. Using inflatable tanks, trucks and cannons, sound trucks whose recorded tank columns carried about 15 miles, and fake "spoof" radio traffic, they staged more than 20 deceptions from Normandy to the Rhine.

Their biggest, Operation Viersen (March 1945), used 600+ inflatable dummies to impersonate two U.S. divisions while the real Ninth Army crossed the Rhine to the north and met little resistance. The unit stayed secret until 1996, and in 2022 Congress awarded it the Congressional Gold Medal.
```
