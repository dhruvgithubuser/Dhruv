# MH370: The Plane That Vanished — shot list
120 s · 72 bpm (beat 0.833 s, bar 3.33 s) · 144 beats · format: 9x16 (1080×1920)
Music: bespoke synthesized score — slow D minor, felt-piano motif, low drone, soft clock-tick pulse; swells at shots 5, 8, 12, end
VO: none — on-screen captions carry the story

## Look: "paper / newsprint"
- Ground: warm newsprint paper (procedural fibre + grain), halftone shading, slight vignette.
- Map: real coastlines (Natural Earth 1:50m via `world-atlas`) drawn in black ink on paper, sea in
  faint halftone dots. A map "camera" pans/zooms in log space between shots.
- Investigation-board devices: red string (the flight path), push-pins, torn paper slips, rubber stamps.
- Faces: **Playfair Display** (headlines, numbers) + **Courier Prime** (typewriter captions, data).
- One accent: string red `#B3261E`. Everything else ink black on paper.
- Plane: generic 777 silhouette in ink. No airline logo, no invented newspaper names, no pilot names.
- Captions: typed on per character (typewriter), max ~14 words per 8-beat shot, ~22 per 12-beat shot.
  Caption slip sits in the top safe area; map in the middle; data cards in the lower third.

## Facts used (checked)
- Departure 00:41 MYT, 8 Mar 2014, KUL→PEK; Boeing 777-200ER 9M-MRO; 227 passengers + 12 crew = 239.
- Last voice transmission 01:19 "Good night Malaysian three seven zero"; transponder lost ~01:21 near IGARI.
- Military primary radar tracked a turn back west across the peninsula; last contact 02:22 NW of Penang.
- Inmarsat handshakes continued until a final partial handshake at 08:19 → the "7th arc"; Doppler (BFO) analysis favoured the southern route.
- 24 Mar 2014: Malaysian PM announced the flight ended in the southern Indian Ocean.
- ATSB-led underwater search ~120,000 km², suspended Jan 2017.
- Flaperon found on Réunion 29 Jul 2015, confirmed from MH370; more debris along East Africa & islands.
- July 2018 MSIT report: the cause could not be determined.
- Ocean Infinity: 2018 search; 2025–26 "no find, no fee" ($70 M) search; phase ended March 2026,
  151 days at sea, >140,000 km² mapped across its searches; CEO: "it isn't where we looked."
  Malaysia extended the contract by a year in June 2026 (not yet resumed as of last reporting).
- Map positions (arcs, last radar, search areas) are drawn approximately and are illustrative.
