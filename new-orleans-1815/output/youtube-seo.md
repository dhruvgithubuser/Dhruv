## Description
```
The Battle of New Orleans was fought two weeks after the peace treaty ending the War of 1812 was signed, because the news was still at sea.

On December 24, 1814, the Treaty of Ghent was signed in Europe. On January 8, 1815, about 8,000 British veterans attacked Andrew Jackson's line outside New Orleans. In just over 30 minutes the British lost 2,037 killed, wounded or captured, including General Sir Edward Pakenham; American casualties were 71. The treaty took effect on February 17, 1815, and the battle made Jackson a national hero and, in 1829, the seventh President of the United States.
```
