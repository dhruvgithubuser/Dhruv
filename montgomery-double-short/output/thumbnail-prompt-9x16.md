# Thumbnail / cover prompt (9:16): Montgomery's Body Double: Did He Fool Hitler? (1944)

Works in ChatGPT, Google Flow (Imagen) or Ideogram. Set **9:16 (portrait)**.

## Prompt (with text)
```
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts cover, photorealistic, like a still from a 1940s war film, black-and-white with a slight warm sepia tint and film grain.

Scene: two identical-looking British generals from 1944 standing side by side, facing the camera: lean faces, sharp nose, small grey moustache, soft black beret with two small badges, khaki battledress. They are mirror twins. The one on the left is lit cleanly; the one on the right is half in shadow, holding a small glass of gin behind his back that only the viewer can see. Background: the Rock of Gibraltar and an airfield, slightly blurred.

Text, perfectly spelled, bold condensed sans-serif capitals with a heavy black outline:
- Top, huge, two lines: "THE FAKE" in white, "GENERAL" in red.
- A red rubber-stamp style box tilted over the right-hand man's chest: "DOUBLE".

Keep both faces fully visible and free of text. No other words, no watermark, no logo.
```

```

```

## Tips
- **"THE FAKE GENERAL"** pairs with the title **"Montgomery's Body Double: Did He Fool Hitler? (1944)"** (lint 98) without repeating its words.
- Montgomery's signature look in 1944 was the **black beret with two badges** (Royal Tank Regiment plus the general officer's badge). Ask for it if the tool draws a peaked cap.
- If a word is misspelled, reply: *"Keep everything the same, only fix the text to read exactly: THE FAKE GENERAL"*.
