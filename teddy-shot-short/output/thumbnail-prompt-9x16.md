# Cover prompt (9:16): Teddy Roosevelt Was Shot, Then Spoke for 50 Minutes

Cinematic movie-poster style, the same as your Mincemeat and Pearl Harbor covers. Set **9:16 (portrait)**.

## Prompt A (recommended)
```
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts cover, hyper-realistic cinematic movie-poster style, dark theatre, high contrast, sharp detail, 35mm film grain.

Centre: Theodore Roosevelt in 1912, a stocky man in his fifties with round wire spectacles and a thick brown walrus mustache, dark three-piece suit with open overcoat, standing at a wooden podium under a single hard spotlight. One hand grips a thick folded speech with a small bullet hole through it. A small dark red stain shows on his white shirt. Defiant expression, jaw clenched, staring straight at the camera.

Behind him: a dark packed 1912 auditorium, faces in shadow.

Text, perfectly spelled, bold condensed capitals with a thick black outline:
- Top, huge, white: "NOT"
- Under it, huge, glowing red: "DEAD YET"
- Bottom, small, gold label: "MILWAUKEE · 1912"

Keep his face completely free of text. No other words, no watermark, no logo, no modern objects, no gore.
```

## Prompt B: short version
```
9:16 cinematic poster photo: Theodore Roosevelt (round spectacles, walrus mustache, dark suit) at a podium in a spotlight, holding a folded speech with a bullet hole, small red stain on his shirt, defiant, dark crowd behind. Text: "NOT" (white) "DEAD YET" (red), small gold "MILWAUKEE · 1912". Only these words, spelled exactly. No watermark.
```

## Negative prompt (if supported)
```
misspelled text, extra words, gibberish letters, watermark, logo, modern clothing, microphone, cartoon, painting style, blurry, deformed hands, extra fingers, gore, heavy blood
```

## Tips
- **Upload a real reference photo:** Roosevelt in Milwaukee that day (`public/assets/archival/car.png`). Say *"base the man on this photo"*.
- **Why "NOT DEAD YET":** it adds the defiance the title doesn't say. It's 3 words, readable at feed size.
- **Keep text in the top two-thirds:** the bottom of a Short is covered by the title and buttons.
