## Method
1. **YouTube autocomplete (US, English):** 19 seed phrases × (seed + a–z) gave 1,633 suggestions; 1,410 were on topic.
   Autocomplete only shows phrases people actually search. A phrase that appears for the bare seed ("rank 0") is more searched than
   one that needs an extra letter. This gives a relative ranking, **not a monthly volume**.
2. **Competition from the live results:** for 80 phrases, the top 20 YouTube results were checked for how many titles contain all
   the phrase's words ("exact"), how many of the top 10 come from big media/history channels ("big"), and what the top 10 typically
   get (median views).
3. **Scores (0–100, estimates):**
   - Demand = 55% autocomplete rank + 45% top-10 median views.
   - Competition = 45% exact titles + 25% big channels + 30% top-10 views.
   - Opportunity = demand − 0.7 × competition.
