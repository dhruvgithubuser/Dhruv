Operation Valkyrie: the 1944 plot to kill Hitler with a briefcase bomb, and why it failed.

On July 20, 1944, Colonel Claus von Stauffenberg placed a bomb under the map table at Hitler's Wolf's Lair headquarters and walked out. At 12:42 it exploded, but the briefcase had apparently been moved behind a heavy table support, and Hitler survived. In Berlin the conspirators launched Operation Valkyrie, an emergency plan they had secretly turned into a coup. By evening Hitler was on the phone, the coup collapsed, and just after midnight Stauffenberg and three fellow officers were shot in the Bendlerblock courtyard. Roughly 200 conspirators were later executed.
