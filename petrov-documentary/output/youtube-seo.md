Stanislav Petrov — the man who saved the world. In 1983 his screen showed 5 American nuclear missiles, and he called it a false alarm.
This is how one man stopped World War 3.

On September 26, 1983, the Soviet early-warning satellite system reported a US missile launch. Procedure said report it up the chain immediately. Petrov doubted it: a real first strike would not start with just five missiles. He reported a system malfunction. He was right — sunlight reflecting off high-altitude clouds had fooled the satellite.

The man who saved the world never fired a shot. He refused to mistake a warning for certainty.
