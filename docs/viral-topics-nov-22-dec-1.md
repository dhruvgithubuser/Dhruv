# Viral Short topics: 22 Nov – 1 Dec 2026 (outside the Excel calendar)

**Method (yt-viral):** I pulled the last ~40 Shorts and ~40 long videos from 15 war and history channels: Simple History, Historigraph, Yarnhub, The Armchair Historian, Imperial War Museums, History Hit, Kings and Generals, Epic History, The History Chap, Mark Felton, HISTORY, Weird History, BazBattles, Biographics and History Matters. That's **930 videos**. Each video is ranked by **how many times it beat its own channel's median** (raw views would just rank channel size). I then checked YouTube search and autocomplete for each candidate, scored every hook with yt-script and linted every title with yt-package.

> The "formula" labels come from the titles; they say what the title does, not why a video worked.

## How to use these with your calendar
- The Excel calendar already has one Short a day. Either **post these as a second daily Short** (for example 7:00 AM IST, which is 8:30 PM EST the night before, the US evening), or **swap out** the calendar Shorts with low search demand.
- **Anniversary-locked**, so make these first:
  1. **Blackbeard** (22 Nov);
  2. **Tootsie Rolls** (26/27 Nov);
  3. **Toulon** (27 Nov);
  4. **Simo Häyhä** (30 Nov).
- **Biggest upside:** **#1 Tootsie Rolls** (19.5M proven, and Thanksgiving week brings the US audience with the highest RPM) and **#3 Violet Jessop** (huge long-form demand, no good Short yet).
- **Accuracy flags to keep in the script:**
  - Häyhä's kill count is disputed, so say "credited with over 500" rather than an exact number.
  - Jessop was a stewardess on the Titanic and a nurse on the Britannic.
  - The Tootsie Roll story: the code name and the candy airdrop are well documented, but say "according to Marine veterans" about specifics such as plugging fuel lines.
