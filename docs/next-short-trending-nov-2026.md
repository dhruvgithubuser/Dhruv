## Pick: "WWI ended at 11 AM. The generals attacked anyway."

**Why:**
- **Biggest proven demand**: 29M views on the final-minutes theme.
- **Almost no explainer Shorts**: the existing Shorts are footage clips, and the best Gunther Short has 412 views.
- **Peak timing**: Veterans Day traffic, plus Q4 ad rates (Nov–Dec CPMs are the year's highest). The audience is older US men, a strong RPM demographic.
- **Brand fit**: it pairs with your WWI "wrong turn" Short (how WWI started → how it ended).

**Hook:** The war would end at 11 AM. At 10:59, Henry Gunther charged a German machine gun.

**Facts to build on** (sourced and hedged in the script):
- The Armistice was signed about 5:10 AM on 11 Nov 1918, to take effect at 11:00 AM.
- Commanders kept attacking. About 2,738 men died on the last day and nearly 11,000 were casualties (Persico, *Eleven Th Month, Eleventh Day, Eleventh Hour*).
- Pvt. Henry Gunther, 313th Infantry, was killed at 10:59 AM, about 60 seconds before peace. He is generally called the last soldier killed in WWI. Germans reportedly waved him back. He was posthumously restored to sergeant.
- Last Canadian: George Price, 10:58. Last British: George Ellison, about 9:30. Last French: Augustin Trébuchon, about 10:45; French graves were dated 10 Nov.
- Congress later held hearings on the last-day casualties.
