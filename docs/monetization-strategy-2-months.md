## 3. The strategy: Shorts for subscribers, long videos for watch hours and revenue
**Why long-form is where the money is:**
- **Long videos** in history and military topics with a US audience typically earn in the range of a few dollars to about $10 per 1,000 views. These are industry ranges, not promises.
- **Shorts** typically earn cents per 1,000 views.
- So build subscribers with Shorts, and earn from long videos.

### A. Shorts (subscriber engine): 1 a day, never 2 on one day
- **Fixed time:** 9:30 PM IST = 11:00 AM EST.
- **Weekly mix:** 3 cut from that week's long video, plus 4 originals from the viral idea banks (British leaders, enemy's-eye, myths, opened graves).
- **Titles:** name or number first, then the twist (*"The Only Soldier to Kill With a Longbow in WWII"*). 3 hashtags in the description, none in the title.
- **First 2 seconds:** the person plus the stakes, with on-screen text that matches the voice. Keep the length 35–55 s and end on a line that loops back to the start.
- **Link every Short to a long video** using the "Related video" field when uploading. This is the main bridge from Shorts to watch hours.
- **Pin a comment** that asks a question. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- **After 48 h, check "Viewed vs swiped away"** (Studio → Shorts). Above 70% is good. Below 60% means the first 2 seconds failed, so change the hook formula next time.

### B. Long videos (watch-hour engine): 1 a week, Saturday 7:30 PM IST (9:00 AM EST)
- **Search-first topics:** people already search for them ("custer last stand documentary", "christmas truce 1914", "pearl harbor warnings"). New channels get found through search before they get recommended.
- **Unique angle every time:** a trial, the enemy's side, or a myth autopsy, never a plain retelling.
- **Length and opening:** 10–12 minutes. In the first 30 seconds, state the promise, with no intro or subscribe pitch.
- **Packaging:** the thumbnail text says what the title doesn't. Test 2 thumbnails with Test & Compare.
- **Playlists:** *Fatal Mistakes* · *Last Stands* · *Saved the World* · *History Myths*. Add an end screen pointing to the next video in the playlist.
- **Compilations, the cheapest source of watch hours:** every 3–4 weeks, combine 15–20 related Shorts into one 15–20 minute long video, such as *"20 Soldiers Who Refused to Die"*. It costs almost nothing and can bring in a lot of watch time.

### C2. How to reach 1 lakh (100,000) views on a video
You won't get 1 lakh on every video, and nobody your size does. The goal is **a breakout every 2–3 weeks**. Each breakout raises the baseline for the next videos. What decides it:
1. **Topic proven at 100k+:** only make topics or formulas that already beat 100k on other channels (enemy's verdict, one man vs an army, the opened grave, the myth). I check each idea against outliers before making it.
2. **The first 2 seconds:** the person plus the stakes, as the first frame and the first spoken words, with text on screen. YouTube widens a Short's audience in rounds. A Short only reaches the next, bigger round if most people **don't swipe away** (aim for 70–80%+) and most watch to the end. Check "Viewed vs swiped away" and "Average percentage viewed" in Studio.
3. **Loop ending:** the last line leads back into the first. Replays raise the percentage viewed above 100%, which is a strong push signal.
4. **Volume and consistency:** 1 a day on 3 platforms gives about 90 chances a month. Breakouts in this niche usually come after a few dozen consistent uploads.
5. **Second chances:** when a strong topic flops (under 300 views), remake it a few weeks later with a new hook and new opening visuals. Don't re-upload the identical file.
6. **For long videos, 1 lakh comes from search plus a breakout Short:** a timely topic (Pearl Harbor week, Christmas Truce), a clean search title, a clickable thumbnail, and a Short linked to it with "Related video".

### D. Earn before YPP
1. **Affiliate links:** in each description, add 1–2 books on the topic (e.g. Amazon Associates).
2. **Buy Me a Coffee or Patreon:** a link in the channel description and the long videos ("support the research").
3. **Sponsorships:** after about 5–10k subscribers. History and education brands (documentary streaming, history apps) sponsor channels this size.
4. **After YPP:** ad revenue, memberships, Super Thanks.
