# Buried Chronicles: trending topics for broad history (Shorts + long videos)

Data was collected live on 11 Nov 2026 (yt-viral method).
- **Sample:** the latest 30 uploads from each of 12 broad-history channels: Simple History, History Matters, Biographics, Weird History, UsefulCharts, Kings and Generals, Epic History, Knowledgia, History Hit, The History Guy, toldinstone and Voices of the Past. That gave 501 videos: 348 long, 153 Shorts.
- **Ranking:** each video is ranked by its **multiple over its own channel's median**, not by raw views, so big channels don't drown out the signal.
- **Search demand:** YouTube autocomplete (US). vidIQ volume scores aren't available (0 credits until 18 Nov).
