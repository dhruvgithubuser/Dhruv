## More ideas
**One man vs the impossible**
6. *Churchill's Wanted Poster: £25, Dead or Alive* (Boer War, 1899). Young Churchill escaped a Boer prison camp, and the Boers put a price on his head. *(Short)*
7. *The General Who Went From Defeat to Victory: Bill Slim in Burma.* He led the longest retreat in British history, then rebuilt that same army and beat Japan in Burma. *(long)*
8. *After Gallipoli, Churchill Went to the Trenches.* The disgraced First Lord of the Admiralty commanded a battalion on the Western Front in 1916. *(Short)*

**Deception**
9. *Montgomery's Body Double Fooled Hitler* (1944). An actor who looked like Montgomery, M. E. Clifton James, was flown to Gibraltar and Algiers to make German spies think no invasion of France was coming. *(Short, the same formula as Mincemeat)*

**The enemy's verdict, and myths**
10. *The Kaiser's Insult That Made Britain's Army Famous.* The BEF called themselves "The Old Contemptibles" after the Kaiser supposedly called them a "contemptible little army". Then the twist: historians doubt he ever said it. *(Short)*
11. *Wellington Called His Own Army "the Scum of the Earth."* He really did, and "Waterloo was won on the playing fields of Eton" is the quote he never said. *(Short)*
12. *Nelson's Most Famous Signal Was a Last-Minute Edit.* "England expects…" was first written "Nelson confides…", and the signal officer changed it because "expects" was quicker to send. *(Short)*

**The commander's fatal mistake (long videos)**
13. *General Gordon: The Relief Army Arrived Two Days Too Late* (Khartoum, 1885). He refused to evacuate and held out for about 10 months; the relief steamers arrived two days after the city fell. *(long, trial or "who's to blame" angle)*
14. *The Worst Day in British Army History: The Somme, 1 July 1916.* About 57,000 British casualties and about 19,000 dead in one day. Haig's plan put on trial. *(long)*
15. *Orde Wingate: Britain's Strangest General.* He led the Chindits behind Japanese lines, and he is famous for strange habits like receiving visitors naked and eating raw onions. *(Short)*
