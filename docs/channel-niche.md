## The niche
> **Buried Chronicles: the one soldier, one decision or one mistake that changed a war.**

- **Topic:** military history, from ancient times to the Cold War. **All countries**, told for an English-speaking (US-first) audience.
- **Angle:** always a person or a single moment: the hero, the gamble, the trick, the blunder. **Never a summary of an event.**
- **Promise:** "The war story you weren't taught."

| 4. **The myth that never happened** | 15% | Napoleon wasn't short, Vikings' horned helmets, Paul Revere's shout, Cromwell executed after death |

### Outside the niche (stop making these)
- Event summaries with no main character (Boston Tea Party, Gold Rush, Halifax).
- Unsolved mysteries (Roanoke) and natural disasters (Pompeii).
- Presidents' trivia or general history facts.

## Format rules
- **Title:** name or number first, then the twist. For example: "189 Men vs 2,000 Soldiers…", "The Man Who Saved the World by Doing Nothing".
- **First 2 seconds:** the person plus the stakes ("One man. 4,000 warriors coming.").
- **Look:** keep one look: the Vox parchment style (maps, photo cards, stamps, bold captions). A recognizable look builds the brand.
- **Schedule:** 1 Short a day at a fixed time (never 2 on the same day), plus 1 long video every 2 weeks that grows out of pillar 3.
- **Series playlists:** *Last Stands* · *Saved the World* · *Fatal Mistakes* · *History Myths*.

## How to know it's working (judge after 30 days, about 30 Shorts)
- The typical Short reaches **2,000+ views** (now about 900).
- In YouTube Studio, the **"Viewed vs swiped away"** figure stays above **70%**.
- Subscribers gained per 1,000 views goes up on pillar 1 and 2 Shorts.
- If one pillar is consistently 2× the others, give it more of the schedule. Don't change the niche itself.
