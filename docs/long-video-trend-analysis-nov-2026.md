## Recommendation: **Chosin Reservoir, 1950** as the next long video
**Why it wins:**
1. **Momentum:** it's the war topic getting the most views *this month*, and the 75th-anniversary window is **open now** (27 Nov – 13 Dec).
2. **Demand:** the median long video on Chosin gets 120K, and the top one 2.7M.
3. **A built-in viral Short to feed it:** *Tootsie Rolls at Chosin* has a **19.5M-view Short** already proven. Make our version as the trailer and link it to the long video.
4. **Money:** a US Marine story, published in December (peak ad season), gives the **highest-RPM audience** the channel can reach.
5. **A unique angle that fits the channel** (Fatal Mistakes, the trial-style storytelling of the Custer video):
   - **The trap MacArthur was warned about.** China "entered the conflict after issuing several warnings" (Wikipedia, citing Roe 2000), MacArthur launched the "Home-by-Christmas Offensive", and about **30,000** UN troops met about **120,000** Chinese soldiers in −30° cold.
   - Tell it from both sides: the Chinese 9th Army's own losses to the cold were catastrophic. That's the enemy's-eye angle that made Midway-from-the-Japanese-side a 19.7M hit.

**Verified facts:** 27 Nov – 13 Dec 1950; ~30,000 UN vs ~120,000 PVA (Appleman 1990; Ye 2007); the "Home-by-Christmas Offensive"; the evacuation from Hungnam ordered 8 Dec.
