## What the 3 winners share
1. **They overturn something.** A body dug up, a famous story that never happened, a famous name that was a typo. Each one makes the viewer think "wait, what?" within a second.
2. **They feel slightly forbidden or morbid.** "Dug up" is the single strongest phrase in the set.
3. **The number or object is concrete:** "141 years", "the bathtub", "the name".

## Why the other 9 stalled
- **The fact is already well known,** e.g. Harrison's long speech, Lincoln's patent, the Tyler grandson (which was in the news in 2025). Viewers swipe past what they already know.
- **There's no twist, just trivia.** A nickname ("Little Magician") or a short retirement offers no curiosity gap.
- **The thumbnails aren't the cause.** Every Short uses the same AI-painting frame with a burned-in caption, and winners and losers look alike. In this series the topic and title did all the work.

## Formats worth borrowing (the format, not the topics)
- **Name-first titles:** "[Famous name]: The [object] That [twist]". The name brings search traffic and the twist brings the click.
- **A numbered series** ("every president") gives you dozens of episodes and binge-watching. Buried Chronicles could run its own series:
  - *Every Napoleon Marshal in 60 Seconds* (it builds on the Napoleon long video);
  - *Kings Who Died Strangely* (European monarchs);
  - *Every War's Last Soldier*.

## Ideas for Buried Chronicles built on the winning triggers (not copies)
**Body or "dug up" (the Zachary Taylor trigger)**
1. *Oliver Cromwell: Executed 2 Years After He Died* (his body was exhumed and hanged in 1661).
2. *Napoleon: Dug Up 19 Years After His Death, Barely Changed* (the 1840 exhumation on St Helena).
3. *Abraham Lincoln: The Gang That Tried to Steal His Body* (the 1876 plot).
4. *Richard III: The King Found Under a Parking Lot* (2012).

**Myth that never happened (the Fillmore bathtub trigger)**
5. *Marie Antoinette Never Said "Let Them Eat Cake"*
6. *Napoleon Wasn't Short: The British Cartoon That Fooled History*
7. *Paul Revere Never Shouted "The British Are Coming"*
8. *Vikings Never Wore Horned Helmets: An Opera Invented Them*

**Name that was a mistake or a disguise (the Grant trigger)**
9. *Why Britain's Royal Family Changed Its Name in 1917* (Saxe-Coburg-Gotha to Windsor, during WWI).
10. *Hitler's Father Was Born Schicklgruber*
11. *Stalin Wasn't His Name; Neither Was Lenin*

## Takeaways for your channel
- The biggest lever in this series was the twist in the title, not the thumbnail style. Test every idea by asking whether the title overturns something the viewer believes.
- Avoid stories that were in the news recently or are already well known (Tyler shows this).
- The winners suggest morbid and myth-busting topics work best for Shorts. Ideas 1, 2 and 6 tie into your Napoleon long video, so the Shorts can point viewers to it.
