**Flops (1.3K–4.6K):** generic "how armies did X" explainers with no villain and no twist. Examples: Mongol siege methods, castle mining, the medieval archer supply chain, Hannibal's ambush method.
**Lesson:** a **named superpower or villain**, a **clever twist** (trap, trick, mistake, fake) and a **humiliation or survival outcome** in the title. Process explainers flop.

### @TheHistoryVault0.7 (Shorts): 30.8K subscribers, 200 Shorts, median 555, best 2.2K (4×)
- The best do one of three things:
  - **Then & Now** (Stalingrad, El Alamein, Remagen, Saint-Nazaire)
  - **A paradox** ("The Naval Battle Where Ships Never Met", "They Trained for Weeks. They Still Missed by 800 Feet")
  - **Raised from the depths / lost and found**
- Every title is stuffed with ", WWII, World War II, WW2, WWII Documentary". **Don't copy that.** It didn't rescue their weak Shorts (their own Ghost Army Short got 36 views).
- Their Shorts ceiling is low: your best Short (Petrov, 1,251) already sits near their best. Ravelin's long-form formula is the stronger model to learn from.

### The five formulas to use (tags below)
- **F1 · Genius trap/trick:** "The Genius Trap That Destroyed…", "The Trick That…" (ravelin's 35–38×)
- **F2 · The day a superpower lost:** "The Day [Empire] Lost an Entire Army" (10.7×)
- **F3 · Underdog numbers:** "How 150 Men Held Off 4,000…" (5.4×)
- **F4 · Unlikely hero/villain:** "The Teenager / Fake General / Traitor Who…" (4–5×)
- **F5 · Paradox or mistake:** "The Mistake That Cost…", "The War That Lasted 38 Minutes" (Vault's best Shorts, ravelin 5.4×)

S = Short (35–60 s) · L = long video (8–15 min). Figures in brackets are the facts to verify and hedge in the script.

## 3. Engagement rules taken from the data
1. **Name the villain or superpower in the title** (Britain, Rome, Napoleon, Hitler). It's ravelin's common thread.
2. **Add the twist word:** trap, trick, mistake, fake, only, never.
3. **Put a number in the title when one exists:** 150 vs 4,000; 38 minutes; 29 years.
4. **No keyword stuffing in titles.** Put hashtags in the description.
5. **End every Short on a question,** and pin a comment that asks it again.
6. **One Short a day at a fixed time** (see the channel audit).
