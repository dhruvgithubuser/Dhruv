## Description
```
He was wounded 9 times, and wrote: "Frankly, I had enjoyed the war."

Sir Adrian Carton de Wiart VC (1880–1963) quit Oxford to fight in the Boer War and took a bullet in the stomach. In Somaliland in 1914 he was shot twice in the face, losing an eye and part of an ear. On the Western Front in 1915 a shell shattered his left hand; when the doctor refused to cut off his fingers, he pulled them off himself. At the Somme he won the Victoria Cross, then was shot through the skull, ankle, hip, leg and ear, and went back every time.

In 1941, aged 60, his bomber crashed into the sea off Libya. He swam ashore, was captured, tunnelled for seven months in an Italian castle prison, and once stayed free for eight days disguised as a peasant: 62 years old, one eye, one hand, and no Italian.
```
