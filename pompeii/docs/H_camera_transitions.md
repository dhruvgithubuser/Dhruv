# H. Camera movements and transitions

## Grammar
- Default join is a **dissolve** (0.5-1.2 s): documentary pacing, continuous time.
- **Hard cuts** only on impacts and shocks (earthquake, eruption blast, collapses) so the cut itself is the hit.
- **Dip to black** marks a change of mode (into evidence or science sections).
- **Whip** (both shots on screen, sliding) for the jump from map to street.
- **Wipe** with a gold divider for the lava/PDC comparison and the then/now sequence.
- Camera moves are slow eased pushes, pulls, dollies and tilts (ease-in-out sine). Zooms read as dolly moves. Shake only on seismic or impact moments, decaying within ~1 s.
- Continuity: adjacent shots share light state (golden > storm > ash > dark > grey > golden > modern > sunset) and screen direction (Vesuvius sits upper-left/centre in every aerial).
