# L. Thumbnail concepts

Rendered with the film's own engine at 1280x720: `deliverables/thumbnails/thumb_1.png`, `thumb_2.png`, `thumb_3.png` (`node pompeii/tools/shoot_thumbs.mjs`). They follow the channel's packaging: a small-caps kicker, one big gold italic word or phrase, one focal image, and no more than four words of real text.

## 1. "They had one day" (recommended for launch)
- **Image:** low angle down the street. The eruption column towers over the arch, and the fictional family is seen from behind, small against it.
- **Hierarchy:** column (eye goes up first), then the gold **Pompeii**, then the kicker **THEY HAD ONE DAY**.
- **Why it works:** human scale against an overwhelming threat; the curiosity gap is "what happened next".
- **Premium version prompt:** low-angle view down a Pompeian street at amber dusk, a colossal grey-white Plinian eruption column rising above a stone arch and the cone of Vesuvius, a Roman family (man, woman, child) seen from behind in the foreground, falling ash, hand-painted semi-realistic documentary illustration, strong silhouette, empty upper third for text.

## 2. "Buried in a day" (then / now split)
- **Image:** the same street split down the middle by a gold line: AD 79 (painted, crowded, roofs) against TODAY (ruins, twin-peaked Vesuvius).
- **Hierarchy:** the split (instant read), then **Buried in a day**, then AD 79 / TODAY.
- **Why it works:** a transformation thumbnail, readable at phone size; it signals that the video shows the real site, not only drama.

## 3. "The city that disappeared" (map / aerial)
- **Image:** aerial over the bay as the column rises; a red arrow points at the town, labelled POMPEII.
- **Hierarchy:** column, then arrow and label, then the gold **Disappeared** with the small kicker THE CITY THAT.
- **Why it works:** matches the channel's map and arrow style (as in "Why everyone wanted Istanbul" and "Do all roads lead to Rome?").

## A/B plan
Launch with #1. If CTR falls below the channel median after 48 h, swap to #2 (YouTube Test & Compare can run all three). Keep the title fixed while testing thumbnails.

## Checks done
- Text is legible at 160 px wide (phone search result).
- No graphic imagery and no bodies, which keeps the video advertiser-friendly.
- No more than 4 words per thumbnail; nothing in the bottom-right corner (YouTube's timestamp sits there).
