## Settings
- **Visibility:** Private (nothing goes live until you have checked the title, thumbnail and chapters)
- **Advanced settings:**
  - Category: Education
  - Video language and title/description language: English
  - Tags (channel-wide defaults): buried chronicles, history, history documentary, ancient history, ancient civilizations, history explained
  - Comments: On; hold potentially inappropriate comments for review
  - License: Standard YouTube License
