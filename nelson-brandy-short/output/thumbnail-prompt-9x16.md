## Tips
- Keep his famous look: the **bicorne hat with the jewelled plume** and the **star medals**. That's what makes Nelson recognisable at a glance.
- If a word is misspelled, reply: *"Keep everything the same, only fix the text to read exactly: DON'T DRINK IT."*
- Don't show his body in the barrel. The text does the shock, and it keeps the video safe for ads.
