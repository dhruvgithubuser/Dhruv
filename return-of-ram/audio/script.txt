Fourteen years had passed. Fourteen years of exile, sacrifice, pain... and an unwavering promise.

But tonight, Ayodhya was waiting for its king.

After defeating Ravana and fulfilling his destiny, Lord Ram began his journey home, accompanied by Sita and Lakshman.

When the people heard that Ram was returning, the entire kingdom prepared to welcome him. Every home became a place of celebration.

But the diya was more than a lamp. It represented the light of knowledge, the strength of hope, and the victory of good over evil.

And then, the moment Ayodhya had waited for finally arrived. Their beloved Ram had returned.

One lamp became thousands. Darkness surrendered to light. And Ayodhya transformed into a celebration that would live for generations.

It was not merely the return of a king. It was the reunion of a family, the fulfillment of a promise, and the restoration of righteousness.

That is why Diwali is more than a celebration. It reminds us that even after the longest darkness, truth, courage and dharma can guide us towards light.

And perhaps this is the deepest message of the festival: light is not just something we place outside our homes. It is something we must keep alive within ourselves.

From that legendary night, the lights of Diwali became a timeless reminder: darkness may surround us, but it never has to define us.

May the light of Shri Ram illuminate every heart. Wishing you and your family a blessed Badi Diwali.

Jai Shri Ram.
